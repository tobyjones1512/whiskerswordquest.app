Spelling words

Focus sounds

Questions that use these sounds come first. Leave all off for the whole year group's content.

Sounds to blend

Pick the sounds to practise. Words that use them are chosen for the round (at least 3 are needed). Leave all off for a mix.

Word sets

Each round uses one of the sets you tick. Leave all off to use the year group's usual sets.

Pairs

3 to 8 pairs. Up to 6 are used in each round.
