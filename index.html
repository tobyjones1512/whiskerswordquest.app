Whiskers'Word Quest

Hello! Let's play with words.
Every right answer wins a fish!

Grammar, spelling and phonics games for children aged 5 to 11. Short rounds, a cheerful cat, and questions written for each school year.

See the games For Education
iPhone iPhone Duo iPad iOS 17 and newer

Learn, then play

Six learning topics and six games, all levelled for ages 5 to 11.

Rewards that keep them coming back

  • Fish for every right answer, with bonus fish for streaks of right answers
  • A new Daily Challenge every day and a weekly goal of 200 fish
  • Outfits for Whiskers and a stamp book to fill
  • Up to 8 children, each with their own year group and progress

Made for every reader

  • Every question can be read aloud
  • Dyslexia settings: easier-to-read letters, wider spacing and coloured backgrounds
  • Colours for red–green and blue–yellow colour blindness
  • Reduce motion, slower games and larger text

For grown-ups

  • A PIN-locked grown-ups area with progress for each child
  • Add your own spelling words from school
  • Syncs between your family's devices with iCloud

For classrooms

  • Classroom mode on iPad: one shared class, no names or profiles
  • Set up a whole class of iPads with one QR code
  • Create tasks on this website and share them as a link or QR code

For Education

Speaks your English

Choose the English your child learns at school. It sets the spellings, the words, the school year names and the reading voice.
