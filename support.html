Common questions

I forgot the grown-ups' PIN

On the PIN screen tap "I forgot my PIN", answer the sum, then choose a new PIN. Progress is kept.

How do I unlock the whole game?

One game is free. Open Grown-ups and the buy button unlocks every game with a single purchase, no subscription. Use "Restore purchases" on a new device.

How do I add another child?

The first child is included. Extra children are an optional purchase in Grown-ups, up to eight in total.

How do I add my child's spelling words or a task?

In Grown-ups you can type your own words for each game, or tap Create a task to make one and save it to My Tasks on your child's home screen. Teachers can make shareable tasks on the Create a Task page.

Does progress sync?

Yes, between devices signed in to the same iCloud account. Check that iCloud is on for the app in Settings.

The sound is too loud, too quiet or I want it off

The speaker button on the home screen turns the voice on or off. Music and sound effects are in Grown-ups. Everything can also be read aloud or slowed down in the accessibility settings.
